# Privacy Policy for ReVisit – Smart Place Alerts

**Last updated: June 2, 2026**

ReVisit ("the app") is developed and operated by Danny Thota. This policy explains what data
the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

---

## What We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign in with Apple, we receive:
- Your **name** (first and last)
- Your **email address** (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide it)
- An **Apple User ID** — a unique identifier Apple provides to identify your account

This information is used solely to back up and sync your saved places across your devices
via iCloud/CloudKit. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

### Location
ReVisit uses your **precise location** in the background to detect when you are near a
saved place. Location data is processed on-device to trigger geofence checks. It is not
stored on our servers or shared with any third party.

### Usage and Diagnostic Data
To improve app quality, ReVisit sends anonymous performance and usage events to
[Sentry](https://sentry.io). This includes:
- App crashes and errors
- Notification pipeline performance (e.g. whether a notification fired, anonymous quality scores)
- In-app feedback (thumbs up / thumbs down on notifications)

This data is **not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID**. It cannot be used to identify you.

## How We Use Your Data

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Name, Email, Apple User ID | Account creation, CloudKit sync |
| Location | Detecting proximity to saved places |
| Usage & diagnostic data | Improving notification quality and fixing bugs |

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or
marketing purposes.

Third-party services used by the app:
- **Apple CloudKit** — stores your saved places and account data. Governed by
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Sentry** — receives anonymous crash and performance data. Governed by
  [Sentry's Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).
- **OpenWeatherMap** — receives your approximate location to fetch weather conditions.
  Governed by [OpenWeather's Privacy Policy](https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy).
- **Anthropic Claude API** — receives place names and condition descriptions to evaluate
  whether notification conditions are met. No personal identifiers are included in these
  requests. Governed by [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy).
- **Serper** — used to search for live information (hours, menus, event dates). Queries
  contain place names only, no personal data. Governed by
  [Serper's Privacy Policy](https://serper.dev/privacy-policy).

---

## Data Retention

Your account data in CloudKit is retained until you delete your account or revoke ReVisit's
access via **Settings → Apple ID → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID**.

Anonymous diagnostic data in Sentry is retained per Sentry's standard retention policy
(90 days by default).

---

## Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by:
1. Revoking access in **Settings → Apple ID → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID → ReVisit → Stop Using Apple ID**
